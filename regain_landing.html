Questions, answered

Is Regain free?

Yes. The core app is free, including the focus timer, app blocking, and screen time insights. Regain Pro unlocks advanced features like premium themes and additional strict-blocking options.

Which platforms does Regain support?

Regain is available on both Android (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store).

What makes Strict Mode different from other blockers?

Once you start a focus session in Strict Mode, you can't end it early or uninstall Regain until the timer runs out. There is genuinely no loophole. That's the point.

Can I block just Reels and Shorts without blocking the whole app?

Yes, that's one of Regain's signature features. Keep Instagram and YouTube usable for messages and search while the infinite-scroll feeds stay blocked.

What are Focus Groups?

Study groups you join with friends or other students preparing for the same exam. Everyone focuses together, and the leaderboard keeps you accountable.
